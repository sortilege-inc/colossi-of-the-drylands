Aberrations

A colossus does not rise alone. Kudamat crafts droves of accompanying aberrations alongside each Child of Godfell, set to guard the soul shard with it.

They do not stay put. Kudamat is raising ever more of them, and they roam the desert between the outposts — one of the first things anyone in the Drylands will tell you has changed.
