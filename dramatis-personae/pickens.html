Dramatis Persona

Pickens

they/them

Someone known only as Pickens (they/them) runs Havalaugh, a mile of tents on the eastern side of Godfell Mountain with not a permanent building in sight. Under them the outpost is fairly receptive to outsiders, hosts the premier marketplace on its side of the mountain, and throws a High Summer Festival famous enough to nearly quintuple the town's size for a week.

There's no sheriff in Havalaugh. Anyone who harms a citizen is never heard from again.
