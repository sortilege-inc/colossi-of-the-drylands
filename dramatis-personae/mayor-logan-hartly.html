Dramatis Persona

Mayor Logan Hartly

she/her

The mayor of Wyllin's Gulch. She put out the Help Wanted notice the posse answered, and gave them the contract to bring down the colossus to the north.

She sent them off with travel packs: ammunition, a medicine kit, rope, lanterns, rations for the steeds, health potions and bandages. She also told them that Destry has hired groups to guard the colossus.
