Dramatis Persona

Trace Harlan

they/them

Trace Harlan (they/them) leads the "Harlan Family", a small but active posse of thieves working out of Wyllin's Gulch for one purpose: to stop ambitious tycoons from mining and producing essentia batteries. They hope to one day tear the factories from the walls of the mesa and drive out the greedy moguls that put profit over people.
