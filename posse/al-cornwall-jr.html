Player Character

Al Cornwall Jr.

He/him

Clothes that are surface level high society, with a lot of scuffs and obvious wear. Eyes like deep ocean blue. A body that's standard halfling height, perhaps a tad stocky. The colour of tan skin, dark hair with premature greys. An attitude like a con with a heart of gold.

Heritage

Halfling, of a Slyborne community.

Scoundrel: You have advantage on rolls to negotiate with criminals, detect lies, or find a safe place to hide.

Rogue — Syndicate

Well-Connected: When you arrive in a prominent town or environment, you know somebody who calls this place home. Give them a name, note how you think they could be useful, and choose one fact from the following list:

• They owe me a favor, but they’ll be hard to find.

• They’re going to ask for something in exchange.

• They’re always in a great deal of trouble.

• We used to be together. It’s a long story.

• We didn’t part on great terms.

The sheet

Rogue (Syndicate) · Slyborne Halfling

Armor — Gambeson Armor, base score 3, thresholds 5/11, Flexible: +1 to Evasion.

Domain cards

Inspirational Words (Grace): Your speech is imbued with power. After a long rest, place a number of tokens on this card equal to your Presence. When you speak with an ally, you can spend a token from this card to give them one benefit from the following options:

• Your ally clears a Stress. • Your ally clears a Hit Point. • Your ally gains a Hope. When you take a long rest, clear all unspent tokens.

Deft Deceiver (Grace): Spend a Hope to gain advantage on a roll to deceive or trick someone into believing a lie you tell them.

Experiences

Let me con and/or grift for a bit · A thief in the night, a dog on the run

Inventory

A Small Dagger and a Dagger.
