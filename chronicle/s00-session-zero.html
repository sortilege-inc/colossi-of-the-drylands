Session 0

Session Zero

2026-09-24

The table set the four up as people who know of each other. They have crossed paths and owe each other a thing or two, but they are not a party yet. The mayor's Help Wanted notice is what puts them on the road together.

The posse

  • Temperance Blacksand — a Cinderkin, awake less than a year, riding a giant scorpion.
  • Paul "The Rev" Wilkins — a preacher whose god's followers carry ritual coins made of essentia. Rides Rex, a giant centipede.
  • Al Cornwall Jr. — small, quick-handed, and a swindler. Rides William, a giant goat.
  • Grish Hayman — a katari guardian who went to the Cinderline after essentia in the sands.

Connections

  • Temperance and Grish. Grish was at The Cinderline looking for essentia when he saw Temperance coming awake. A crew was trying to break up and mine the ground Temperance was rising out of, and Grish ran them off. Then he got too close and a geyser went up under him. Temperance took most of the heat; without them his face would have melted. Temperance, lost and walking into danger, went with Grish, who took them in.
  • Temperance and the Rev. Temperance asked that if they die in a fight, the Rev grind them to dust and scatter them on the Cinderline. He agreed to perform the rites. Temperance asked it plainly, as a practical matter.
  • The Rev and Temperance. The Rev gave Temperance a ritual coin of essentia bearing the mark of his god, as a blessing. It also means they will meet again.
  • Temperance and Al. Temperance told Al they do carry some essentia, and cannot work magic at all.
  • Al and Temperance. Temperance watched Al win a drinking contest for a pile of cash or essentia, using sleight of hand rather than drinking. Asked how someone so small out-drank someone so big, Al said he hit his head as a kid and can't get drunk. Temperance believes it.
  • Al and the Rev. Al talked the Rev into stealing a keg in a town neither of them can remember. They were arrested and woke up somewhere outside town. The Rev knows Al's story about not getting drunk is a lie.
  • Al and Grish. After watching Grish fight, Al gave him the handle of his beer stein.

The road north

The posse turns north out of Wyllin's Gulch toward the colossus. They are not the only ones on that road. Other groups are heading the same way, and some have already reached the colossus and dug in to defend it from anyone who means to destroy it.
