Session 1

The Glass Snake

2026-10-07

The posse rode north out of Wyllin's Gulch on the mayor's contract, toward the place she had marked as their quarry. Under two suns, the ride was put at less than two days.

The first night

Riding out, the Rev saw water in the distance and went after it with his dowsing rods. It was a mirage. He got twenty yards before he knew it, and Grish, keeping close behind him, gave himself a headache staring at the same nothing.

At camp they divided the mayor's travel pack. Grish doesn't use guns and gave away the revolver and rifle ammunition; Al took it. The Rev took the medicine kit. Temperance took the lanterns, the steeds' rations and the bandages.

Al taught Temperance a card game he called Seven Card Roundup, played with a deck of jokers. Temperance couldn't touch the cards without burning them, so they called their plays out loud. They saw through Al's scoring and needled him about it. Al denied everything and dealt again.

Grish whittled a stick of Joshua tree to keep his claws sharp while the Rev talked about the company. The Rev means to bring the colossus down to show people they don't need the company to keep them safe. Grish asked how people would survive without it. The Rev has lived on this land his whole life, and says they were fine before the company came and will be fine when it leaves. Asked who he serves, he said this land, and whoever saved his life.

Grish told the Rev how he met Al: a four-against-one bar fight, after which Al found him with the handle of a beer stein. The handle is still tied to Grish's armor.

Temperance offered the Rev back the coin he had given them. He told them to keep it, since they still had their head on their shoulders. Temperance slept under their scorpion, which has learned to come to them for the heat.

Spiders

In the night the steeds woke everyone. A swarm of spiders had come for the food and the fire. They were driven off with flame, fists, and a Bolt Beacon from the Rev into the thickest of them, which left spider all over William, Al's goat. Al kept watch for the rest of the night.

They took the rest of the night as a long rest.

  • Temperance started teaching their scorpion not to kill them, letting it close its pincers on them and flaring heat until it stopped.
  • Temperance practised with the boomerang, a hunting boomerang made to break legs. It doesn't come back to them yet.
  • Grish shadowboxed, using Temperance as a post.
  • Al got used to the gun and worked on an accent: Alex, from Richmond.
  • Grish started work on his tracking, and found the spider nests he had missed when he made camp.
  • The Rev ran from rock to rock rehearsing his channelling.

The cliff

The next afternoon a cliff blocked the way north, with old pitons in its face. Grish climbed claws-out and set rope. Al rode William straight up it. Temperance climbed until they noticed wave-shaped marks in the rock, followed them sideways, and found a wide hole something had bored out of the cliff. They asked the Rev whether this was what their key was for. "Have to be a damn big key." The Rev's centipede wanted nothing to do with the hole.

A snake of jagged glass came out of it. Temperance lit their boomerang, went Unstoppable and hit it. It wrapped round them and struck, and Unstoppable took the edge off every hit. Grish roped it round the neck and held on while it tried to pull him over the edge. Al shot it from the top. The Rev prayed, jumped from his saddle with his broadsword, missed it by inches and fell. Al caught his ankle with a grappling hook and held him upside down against the rock.

When the snake bit Temperance, it bit through the crust into the heat. The front of its jaw melted like glass. It fell to the foot of the cliff and shattered, and half the rope went with it.

Grish hauled the Rev up. At the top, Temperance: "It seems the glass snakes get a little feisty."

The oasis

From the top of the cliff they could see steel roofs in the distance, an outpost none of them knew. The mayor had told them that Destry, who runs the biggest mining factory in Wyllin's Gulch, has hired groups to guard the colossus.

Then the Rev saw water again, and this time it was there. He rode a mile and a half the other way to The Moving Oasis, the Lost Oasis of the rumors, which he had found once before. Grish walked its shore and found it was no monster. Temperance waded in and stopped at the ankles in a cloud of steam. The Rev swam.

They rested there. The Rev took it as a sign: he hadn't been leaping at the snake, he said, he had been sensing the oasis.
