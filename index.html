Nine shards of a buried god, and nine colossi standing over them.

A Daggerheart Chronicle

In the center of the world there is a mountain, and deep beneath that mountain there is an ancient and terrible god struggling to break free. Most folk in the Drylands took that for a tall tale — right up until the ground outside Wyllin's Gulch split open and something the size of a mesa climbed out of it. This is the archive of the outfit riding out to meet the rest of them.
