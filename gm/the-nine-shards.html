SOURCE Drylands History — the New Gods split part of Kudamat's being into nine pieces and tasked nine mortals with hiding them. The riders buried them in the arid earth as far from the mountain as they could manage. Kudamat reaches for them through veins of essentia; each one he reaches, he guards with a colossus and droves of aberrations.
