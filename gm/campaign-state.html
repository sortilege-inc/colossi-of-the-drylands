Campaign State — as of Session 1

☩
Behind the Veil — GM material. Spoilers and prep. Players of the Drylands, turn back.

SET decided at the table · SOURCE verified in the frame material · OPEN undecided · NOTE caution

1. The situation

SOURCE Drylands History — Kudamat has recovered one of nine soul shards. He is siphoning power through the essentia veins, raising aberrations across the desert, and searching for the remaining eight. Each shard he reaches gets a colossus set over it.

SET The posse is on Mayor Logan Hartly's contract to bring down the colossus north of Wyllin's Gulch, under two days' ride. They answered her Help Wanted notice separately.

SET Other outfits are riding for the colossus too. Some have already dug in to defend it, among them the groups Baron Ryder Destry has hired to guard it.

SET The Drylands have two suns.

2. Shards and colossi

Tally and per-shard detail: The Nine Shards.

Recovered by Kudamat: 1 of 9. Colossi standing: 1 — The First Colossus, north of Wyllin's Gulch, guarding the recovered shard.

OPEN Where the other eight riders buried their shards, and in what order Kudamat reaches them.

3. Who is moving

  • Mayor Logan Hartly — holds the posse's contract. SET
  • Baron Ryder Destry — runs the biggest mining factory in Wyllin's Gulch; has hired groups to guard the colossus. SET
  • Preacher Rayos, The Harlan Family, Iron Tusk Corp, The Drylands Law — in the frame, not yet met. SOURCE

OPEN Whether "the company" the Rev means to free people from is Destry's operation.

4. The posse

Temperance Blacksand, Paul “The Rev” Wilkins, Al Cornwall Jr. and Grish Hayman. Connections: Session Zero.

SET Temperance formed in the hour the first colossus rose, half a desert away, and carries trace essentia. Others of their kind exist, few and far between.

SET When Grish found Temperance coming awake at The Cinderline, a crew was mining the ground they were rising out of.

OPEN What made Temperance, and whether the answer is Kudamat, the New Gods, or neither. They do not know.

5. Where Session 1 stopped

SET Rested at The Moving Oasis, a mile and a half off the road north, on the far side from an outpost with steel roofs that none of them knows. Half the rope was lost with the glass snake.
