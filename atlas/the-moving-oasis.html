The Moving Oasis

The Lost Oasis of the rumors. The Rev has found it twice, the second time from the top of a cliff on the road north, a mile and a half off the line.

Grass and green trees, in the middle of the desert. At the water's edge grow many-coloured succulents like cactus, which have no business next to water, along with Venus flytraps. Butterflies everywhere, and animals that are plainly not desert animals. The water is clear, sparkling and cold, nothing like the tepid standing water anywhere else in the Drylands.

It is about half a mile around. Grish walked the whole shore and satisfied himself it is no monster and no aberration.

It does not stay put. Whether there are many oases or one that turns up when it is most needed, the Rev doesn't know. He takes it as a blessing.

At the table: the Rev finds it when his water table comes up 10 or 20. Whoever is with him gets the benefits of a long rest without the cost one would otherwise carry on the hunt.
