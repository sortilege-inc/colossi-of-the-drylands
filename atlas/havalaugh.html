Havalaugh

The name sounds as cordial as the folks in this encampment. Though there isn't a permanent building in sight, the tents that form this town stretch for at least a mile. Run by someone known only as Pickens, Havalaugh is fairly receptive to outsiders. The outpost is considered to host the premier marketplace on the eastern side of the mountain, and its High Summer Festival is famous — enough for Havalaugh to nearly quintuple its size for a week. There's no sheriff in Havalaugh, but anyone who harms a citizen is never heard from again.
