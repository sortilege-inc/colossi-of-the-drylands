Factions

Factions

Who holds the mines, who holds the law, and who wants the whole business torn off the mesa.

Faction

The Forgotten Gods

The builders of the Mortal Realm, thrown down and buried in it. Their wounds are the source of all magic.

Faction

The New Gods

The usurpers in the Hallows Above, who buried their predecessors and left blighted ground to keep mortals off the graves.

Faction

The Harlan Family

A small but active posse of thieves out of Wyllin's Gulch, robbing the essentia industry rather than the innocent.

Faction

Iron Tusk Corp

Owns the massive mine at the heart of the Iron Kettle — and rather less of the town than it would like.

Faction

The Kettle Trade Union

The smiths' union that actually governs the Iron Kettle, and carries the liability Iron Tusk Corp would rather not.

Faction

The Flock of Preacher Rayos

A congregation carried out of Left Luck Outpost to preach the prophecy in a town that worships gold.

Faction

The Drylands Law

Sheriffs and deputies — often the most powerful people in town, usually in the pocket of the mine moguls, and still the ones most ready when something enormous comes over the ridge.
